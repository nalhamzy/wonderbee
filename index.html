A new learning adventure

Little puzzles.
Big discoveries.

Explore oceans, space, gardens, dinosaurs, the body, and our wild Earth with Pip the bee. Short narrated discoveries become simple puzzles, then more challenging recall and sentence-building games.

Curiosity grows one level at a time

Wonderbee contains 48 discoveries in six worlds. Children start with guided choices, progress toward independent challenges, and revisit discoveries in Memory Mix. There are no countdowns, lost lives, ads, or child accounts.

Try the first two discoveries in every world for free. An optional, parent-only, one-time Explorer Pack unlocks the remaining content. Its current local price appears in the store purchase screen. Completing a purchase opens content; children still progress through the levels.

The native iOS and Android release is being prepared. This page does not claim the app is already available in either store.

English narration is AI-generated and pre-recorded. It is not a real child's voice. All game content and narration are bundled with the app; store purchases and restoration need internet access.
