Privacy for curious families

Updated 9 October 2026. Wonderbee is a child-directed educational game from Ideal Intelligence for ages 6–8 with a parent's guidance. Contact: contact@idealailabs.com.

Learning stays on the device

Completed discoveries, stars, review history, and sound settings are stored locally. We do not ask for a child's name, email, date of birth, photo, voice, or location. There is no child account, social chat, advertising, advertising identifier, microphone recording, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics SDK.

Game images and AI-generated narration are bundled with the app. No child's speech or learning answers are sent to an AI service. We do not create personalized child profiles.

Optional parent purchases

An adult gate protects purchases, restoration, and external links. Apple or Google handles payment using the parent's store account. We do not receive card details.

When a parent buys or restores Explorer Pack, the app sends the store transaction identifier or purchase token product identifier, and a random installation identifier to our purchase verification service, hosted on Google Cloud, to check ownership with Apple or Google. These purchase records are used for app functionality and fraud prevention, not advertising or learning analytics. The verification service does not store a database of raw receipts, purchase tokens, or installation identifiers. Proof of ownership is stored securely on the device. Cloud operational logs follow the hosting provider default retention; we do not intentionally log purchase request payloads. Network services may process an IP address and technical request logs to operate and secure the service. They are not used to track children across apps.

We do not sell personal data. Purchase information is shared only with the applicable app store and service providers operating verification for us. Apple and Google apply their own privacy policies to store accounts and payments.

Control and retention

Parents can reset learning progress inside the parent area or remove the app to erase its local data. Store purchase records remain subject to Apple's or Google's retention requirements. Resetting progress does not erase a purchase or grant a refund. Contact us to ask about any information processed by our verification service; we may need the store transaction reference to locate it. Do not include a child's personal information.

Support and this website

If a parent contacts support, we receive the information they voluntarily send and use it to address their request. This website uses GitHub Pages hosting and no advertising or analytics scripts. Hosting providers may process basic security logs when a browser requests a page.

Updates

Material changes will be reflected here with a new date. We will not add child accounts, advertising, or new uses of children's personal information without updating the app's design and applicable parental controls.
